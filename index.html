What NextSteps Is

NextSteps is an idea for a web tool that would take the notes or transcript from a meeting and help turn them into an actual list of tasks that need to get done. Instead of leaving a meeting with a page of notes that nobody really looks at again, the point of this tool would be to pull out the action items and turn them into something people can actually use the next day.

The Problem

Most meetings end with someone typing notes into a document, a group chat, or sometimes just a notebook. Those notes usually mix together decisions, ideas, and things that people are supposed to do, all in one long block of text. A few days later, it can be hard to remember who was supposed to do what, or when it was actually due. Important tasks end up buried in paragraphs that nobody re-reads.

The Basic Idea

The idea behind NextSteps is to take those same meeting notes and sort out the useful information automatically. Instead of one long block of text, the notes would be broken down into separate tasks, who is responsible for each one, and when it is due. Bigger tasks could also be split into smaller steps so they feel less overwhelming to start.

Why Someone Would Use This

This kind of tool could be useful for college students working on group projects, engineering teams, small businesses, or really anyone who leaves a meeting with a lot of notes but isn't totally sure what needs to happen next. Instead of trying to remember everything or re-reading old notes, a person could just check their daily list and know what they are responsible for.
